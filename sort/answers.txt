sort1 uses: Bubble sort

How do you know?: compares pairs of adjacent values one at a time and swaps them if they are in the incorrect order.

sort2 uses: Merge sort

How do you know?: It exhibits recursive behavior and involves dividing the list into halves before merging them back in order.

sort3 uses: Selection Sort

How do you know?: selecting the smallest element each time and moving it to its correct location.
